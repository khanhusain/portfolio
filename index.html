About Me

I'm Husain, from Mumbai, and I'm drawn to software because I like turning a problem into something that actually works. I enjoy the whole journey: understanding the problem, planning a solution, writing the code, and fixing whatever breaks along the way.

I care about code that is clear, simple and easy for the next person to read. I learn by building real things, and I like knowing how my tools work underneath, not just how to use them. Whatever I build, I want it to be useful, reliable and well made.
